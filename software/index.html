MIDI, Audio & Productivity

Music first stole my heart, a melodious love story that never fades. But then, coding whispered its logic, turning passion into an intricate symphony of problem-solving and creation.

Apps for music making: “SPULE”, “CC PAL” and “X/Y PAL”. All three run on iOS.

“SPULE” turns your iPhone into a reel-to-reel tape recorder. Record snippets, put them on virtual cassettes, scrub through takes by turning the reels and shape the sound with tape effects.

“CC PAL” lets you control MIDI signals on your synths and software with just a few taps - up to 16 CC sliders you can record and loop. “X/Y PAL” brings a slick X/Y pad with up to 8 parameters per pad, so you can tweak and twist your sounds in real time. Both play nice with all your gear, whether you’re laying down tracks in your bedroom or bringing the house down on stage.
