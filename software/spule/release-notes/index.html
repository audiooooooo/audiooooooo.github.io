Every SPULE release, newest first.

Version 1.3

New

  • Dark mode: a warm, dim-studio look for late sessions and dark stages. SPULE follows your iPhone’s setting

  • Settings > Appearance: choose System, Light or Dark

  • In dark mode a red LED glow lights the cassette, and it glows brighter while you record

Version 1.2

New

  • Trim samples: drag the frame to choose the part that plays. The original recording stays untouched

  • Fling the waveform to wind the tape fast, and fling again to wind faster

  • Feel and hear every loop pass with a haptic tick and an optional VoiceOver announcement

Improved

  • Reels now turn like real cassette reels

  • Crash-safe recording: if SPULE is interrupted while recording, your take is recovered

Version 1.1.1

Improved

  • Lower battery use while SPULE is idle or playing in the background

  • Less memory use and smoother performance

Version 1.1

New

  • Apply FX: print the effects into the recording while you record, including every fader move. Turn it off to keep the original and use the effects as the sample’s playback effects

  • Record through the effects: Level, Echo, Reverb, Lo-fi and Tape now work on the live input, and you can change them while a take runs

  • Full-length loop takes: a loop recording now saves the whole take, not just the last pass

  • Hear yourself and the loop on headphones while recording, in time with what you play

  • New loop sound controls: Wobble, Wear and Splice

  • Gain for the input level of the recording

  • New Haptic Feedback switch in Settings

Improved

  • Loops from 1 second up to 3 minutes (before: up to 30 seconds)

Version 1.0

New

  • First release of SPULE: a reel-to-reel recorder with interactive reels and reverse playback

  • Six per-sample effects: pitch, level, echo, reverb, lo-fi and tape

  • Tape loop recording of 0.5 to 30 seconds with feedback, wobble and tone

  • Cassettes to organize and share your recordings

  • Recording quality and format options, input selection and optional location tagging

  • Optional iCloud Drive sync of cassettes, recordings and effect settings
