X/Y PAL ~ MIDI and OSC X/Y pad controller for iOS

Download on the App Store

X/Y PAL turns your iPhone or iPad into a live touch controller. Move a finger on an XY pad and X/Y PAL sends MIDI Control Change and OSC to your synths, apps and computer at once – with no delay and no pop-ups while you play.

Version 2.0 is a major update: a new layout, more freedom in how you map the pads, OSC support and a new concept of instruments. Your presets and settings from earlier versions are carried over.

Questions? Support & FAQ · Community

PLAY

  • Two XY pads on iPhone, four on iPad in landscape, each with its own preset

  • Every pad controls eight slots: X1–X4 on the horizontal axis, Y1–Y4 on the vertical axis

  • Play with several fingers on different pads at the same time

  • A touch sends its position at once, and the release value is never lost

  • Switch single slots on or off per pad with their lights

  • Colored lines on the side of each pad show the value every slot is sending

PRESETS

  • Presets name no hardware. Bind a preset to your instruments and it works with any device

  • Factory presets to start with, and your own presets to create, duplicate, rename, save and delete

  • Edit a preset without risk: editing a factory preset makes a copy

  • Synth library with named controls, so you pick CUTOFF instead of CC 74

EDIT A SLOT

For every slot you choose:

  • the instrument and the control it sends to

  • the minimum and maximum value

  • whether the direction is inverted

  • whether it returns to the minimum when you let go

  • its name and color

A test strip in the editor sends a value to the slot’s instrument while you set it up. Hints on the pad show when a slot has no instrument yet, or when two slots send the same control to the same place. Tap a hint to fix it.

INSTRUMENTS, MIDI AND OSC

  • Instruments: set up your own gear once, as a MIDI output with a channel or as an OSC target with an address prefix

  • MIDI Control Change and OSC, scaled to 0–127 for every slot

  • OSC messages in the form /xypal/cc/43 with a value of 0–127, with your own host, port and address prefix

  • Use the MIDI outputs on your device, pair Bluetooth MIDI devices and see which outputs are offline

  • Network MIDI switch: connect a Mac over Audio MIDI Setup and send to it without cables

  • Sending never blocks the touch screen. A slow or missing output cannot stall the pads or your other outputs

  • Haptic feedback on iPhone, which you can switch off in Settings

  • Everything is stored on your device. X/Y PAL has no accounts and no tracking

WHAT CAN I DO AND WHAT NOT

  • You can control synths, drum machines, apps and software with MIDI CC and OSC

  • You can play up to four XY pads at once on an iPad

  • You can build presets once and use them with different instruments

  • You cannot make sound: X/Y PAL is a controller, so you need a synth or app to play

  • You cannot record or sequence your moves: X/Y PAL is for playing live
