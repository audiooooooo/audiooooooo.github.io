FAQ

Does CC PAL make sound?

No. It only generates MIDI and OSC messages. You need a synthesizer, an app or a DAW that receives them.

How do I connect it?

Send MIDI via Bluetooth or USB, or send OSC messages to receivers on your local network.

How many sliders can I have?

Up to 16 sliders per configuration, and you can create as many configurations as you like. Each slider has its own MIDI channel, CC number, color and name.

Are there ready-made configurations?

Yes, for the KORG Volca FM, KORG Volca Beats, KORG minilogue XD and DSI Prophet Rev2.

How do I control Ableton Live?

Connect via Bluetooth MIDI and map the sliders to parameters in your devices. There is a video: CC PAL - Configure MIDI mapping with Ableton Live.

Can I record slider movements?

Yes. Record the movement of each slider and play it back in a loop. A small chart shows which movements are stored in a slider.

How to report a bug

Write to hey [at] audio [dot] ooo and include:

  1. The CC PAL version and your iOS version
  2. Your device model
  3. How you connect: Bluetooth MIDI, USB or OSC, and which synth, app or DAW receives it
  4. What you did, what you expected and what happened instead
  5. The MIDI channel and CC number, or the OSC address, of the slider involved
