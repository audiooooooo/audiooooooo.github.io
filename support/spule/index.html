FAQ

Where are my recordings stored?

On your device first. They are plain audio files you can also see in the Files app. If you switch on iCloud Drive sync in Settings, your cassettes, recordings and effect settings sync across your devices.

Why does SPULE ask for my location?

Only if you want to tag recordings with the place where you made them. This is optional and you can switch it off in Settings under Privacy.

Which microphone can I use?

The built-in microphone, a wired or Bluetooth headset, or a USB or Lightning microphone. Pick the input in Settings.

Which file formats and quality can I record in?

Choose Low, Medium or High quality and the format M4A/AAC, ALAC, WAV or AIFF in Settings. Changes apply to new recordings, not to existing ones.

Can I record the effects into the take?

Yes. Switch on Apply FX to print the effects, including every fader move, into the recording. Switch it off to keep the original recording and use the effects as the sample’s playback effects.

How long can a tape loop be?

From 1 second up to 3 minutes. A loop recording saves the whole take, not just the last pass.

Is SPULE a multitrack recorder?

No. SPULE is a tape recorder, not a DAW. It does not edit in multitrack.

How to report a bug

Write to hey [at] audio [dot] ooo and include:

  1. The SPULE version (shown in Settings) and your iOS version
  2. Your device model
  3. Your audio input and output: built-in, wired, Bluetooth or USB
  4. What you did, what you expected and what happened instead
  5. Whether it happens every time, and whether iCloud Drive sync is switched on

A short screen recording helps a lot.
