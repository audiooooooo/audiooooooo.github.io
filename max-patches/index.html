Max Patches

Patches for Cycling ’74 Max that I build while debugging, testing and making noise. Free to download, open and pick apart.

Little tools for Cycling ’74 Max. Each one started as a patch I needed on my own desk: to see what a controller really sends, to measure how fast, or to poke at an idea before it turns into an app.

Download them, open them in Max, turn off presentation mode and look at how they work. Change them, break them, make them yours.

Ever wondered who's behind the scenes of audio.ooo? Well, it's someone who's been madly in love with audio and MIDI since 1999 - talk about a long-term relationship!
