Resources for learning TouchDesigner

TouchDesigner by Derivative. It is powerful. It’s a node-based visual programming environment primarily used for real-time interactive multimedia content. It’s wonderful for creating installations, live visuals, and interactive experiences.

But where to start? Because TouchDesigner has a growing community there are tons of resources. I try to list the ones I really like and don’t want to forget.

Courses / Books

If you want the whole package of information. These complete video courses and books are a good starting point.

Beginner Course by Bileam Tschepe

An excellent beginner course on the official TouchDesigner YouTube channel. Covers all the fundamentals you need to get started.

Interactive & Immersive HQ Book

A comprehensive free online book that covers TouchDesigner from basics to advanced topics. Great reference material.

Interactive Immersive Courses

Professional courses that take you deeper into TouchDesigner. High-quality content for serious learners.

The NODE Institute

Advanced courses and workshops covering creative coding and immersive media, including TouchDesigner.

Video Content

There are lots of people providing useful TouchDesigner information. Here are some which I really like.

TouchDesigner Official Channel

The official channel with tutorials, showcases, and community content. Always a good place to start.

elekktronaut

Excellent tutorials covering practical techniques and creative approaches to TouchDesigner.

Matthew Ragan

Deep-dive tutorials and technical explorations by Matthew Ragan, who also maintains an excellent learning resource site.

Stanislav Glazov

Great tutorials and tips from this Berlin-based TouchDesigner expert. Also check out his Houdini to TouchDesigner resource.

Documentation / References

Official Learn Derivative

The official learning platform with structured tutorials, examples, and documentation. The best place to understand the fundamentals.

Derivative Forum

The official forum is a massive source of knowledge about TouchDesigner. Great community support and archives.

Community

TouchDesigner Discord

Active Discord community with helpful members ready to answer questions and share projects.

TouchDesigner Subreddit

Reddit community for sharing work, asking questions, and staying updated with the latest news.

Experts & Inspiration

Oliver Ellmers

UK-based TouchDesigner expert with impressive commercial work and installations.

YFX Lab - Roy & Tim Gerritsen

Netherlands-based studio creating cutting-edge interactive installations and visuals with TouchDesigner.
